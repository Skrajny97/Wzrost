Insight Privacy Policy

In force from 12 November 2026.

Who is responsible for your data

The controller of personal data is Karol Wilk, the creator of the game Insight, available at insightrpg.com and as the Insight app on Google Play. Contact: kontakt@insightrpg.com.

What data we collect and why

  • Account: login, e-mail address, password hash (we never store the password itself), game language, the dates the account was made and its e-mail confirmed, and your confirmation that you are at least 16. They serve signing in and running the account.
  • Sign-in with Google: your Google account ID and its e-mail address. We get no password or other data from the Google account.
  • Game: characters (name, look, class), their progress, items, exploration reports, camp, settlement, guild, party, friends, gifts, trades and quests. The game cannot work without them. A character's name, level, class and guild are visible to other players, for example in the Ranking and on the chat.
  • Chat: the text of messages on channels and in private conversations, tags, reports and blocks. Other players see channel messages; a private conversation is seen by its participants. A channel keeps its last 300 messages, a private conversation its last 100, and reported ones wait until they are reviewed. The game's creator reviews reported messages and may hide them.
  • Chat translation: when you tap “Translate”, that message's text goes to machine translation by Cloudflare Workers AI, and the translation is stored with the message. We also count how many translations an account asked for that day.
  • Notifications: when you turn them on, we store your browser's notification subscription address and its keys. Notifications are encrypted and pass through the push service of your browser or phone (for example Google or Apple). Turning notifications off deletes the subscription.
  • Bug reports: the text of a report from “Report a bug”, the screen, the game version, a description of the device (browser and system), the state of the game's notifications on the device (whether they are allowed and on, and the last error in switching them on) and a screenshot if you attach one. Only the game's creator sees them.
  • Account protection: the number of failed sign-in attempts for a login or e-mail, kept for 15 minutes to make guessing passwords harder.
  • Cookies: a session cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days, and a short cookie for the time of signing in with Google. We use no advertising or analytics cookies.
  • Device storage: settings (sound, narrator's voice, animations, fight speed, language) stay in your browser.

Legal basis

Account, game, chat, translations and notifications: performance of the contract for the game service you ask for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Account protection, chat moderation and bug reports: legitimate interest, namely the safety of the game and its players and fixing errors (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Who receives data

  • Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the game and its database, sends the confirmation e-mails and translates chat messages, as a processor under a data processing agreement. Data may be processed outside the European Economic Area under standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Google, when you sign in with Google.
  • Push services of your browser or phone receive only encrypted notifications.
  • Other players see what is public in the game: a character's name, level, class, guild and messages on chat channels.

We do not sell data, show ads or use analytics or tracking tools.

How long

We keep account and game data while the account exists. “Delete account” in the Settings at once deletes the account, its characters and all their data, messages, reports and notification subscriptions. Database backups are gone after 30 days at most. The page Deleting an account says how to delete an account.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected, deleted, restricted and transferred, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to kontakt@insightrpg.com; we answer within a month. You may also complain to the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, uodo.gov.pl), or to the authority of your own country.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are stored only as a PBKDF2 hash, and the session cookie cannot be read by the page's scripts.

Age

The game is for people aged 16 or over. When creating an account, you confirm that you are at least 16. We delete an account made by someone younger as soon as we learn of it.

Changes

We announce changes to this policy in the game, and the date at the top says since when the current version applies.
