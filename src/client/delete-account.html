Deleting an Insight account

In the game

  1. Sign in in the Insight app or in a browser at insightrpg.com.
  2. Tap the cog (Settings) in the top right corner.
  3. At the bottom tap “Delete account”, type your login and confirm with “Delete account”.

Without signing in

If you cannot sign in, write to kontakt@insightrpg.com from the address of the account or give its login. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm it in our answer.

What is deleted

At once: the account with its login, password and e-mail address, the link to the Google account, all characters with their items, reports, camp and settlement, chat messages, reports, notification subscriptions and all sign-in sessions. Database backups are gone after 30 days at most. Nothing is kept.
